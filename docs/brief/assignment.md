## Bounty Hunters

A friendly vibe-coding competition to do useful things.

Bounty Hunters is a friendly competition across the Engineering Division to vibe code a solution to do useful things. Each event has a specific goal. Participation is entirely voluntary; you are not asked to work nights/weekends.

## Kudos Program Event

### Program Goal
Develop a software interface that allows employees to **recognize colleagues for going above and beyond** — e.g. acknowledging teammates for being a great friend, expertly handling difficult clients, or providing critical help during a crisis.

### Implementation & Design
- Participants "vibe code" a software solution to support this kudos system.
- The implementation of a **point system is flexible**, left to the developer's discretion.
- The **integration method is open-ended** (e.g. via Slack); determining how it functions and integrates into work life is **a core part of the challenge**.
- For inspiration, look at the **Nectar** platform.

### Swag & Logistics
- Ultimately reward folks for good work: enable users to **redeem points for swag or gift cards, etc.**
- **Point-redemption details are out of scope** for this hackathon (resolved later).

### Timeline & Resources
- **Project period:** June 30 – July 20, 2026.
- **Allocated time:** none. Participants may use a **maximum of 4 hours of work time** (unallocated time); additional after-hours time is at the participant's discretion. You must continue to work your allocations.
- Participation is not required.

### Judging & Awards
- Present in a **5–10 minute shareout** during a division-wide call (Tue Jul 21 or Wed Jul 22; first-come sign-up).
- If many submissions, a second round with a technical deep dive may be required.
- Winner decided by Engineering Division Leadership.
- **Overall winner:** $250 gift card. **Runner-up:** $100 gift card.
- Winner gets an allocation to see the project through to full implementation.
- **All code submitted in a personal GitHub repo by midnight CST on July 20.**
- Winners announced Friday, July 24.

### Judging Criteria
| Criteria | Description |
|---|---|
| Effectiveness of the Submission | How well the solution solves the problem and delivers value to its intended users. |
| Use of AI During Development | How effectively AI tools accelerated development, improved quality, solved problems, or increased productivity. |
| Ease of Use | How intuitive, engaging, and user-friendly the solution is for its intended audience. |
| Ability to Maintain | How easy the solution is to understand, deploy, support, and enhance after the hackathon. |
| Overall Presentation | How clearly and professionally the demonstration explains the solution. |

## Extracted hard constraints (for our planning)
1. **Recognition of peers** is the core function.
2. **Points system** required, but design is our discretion.
3. **Integration into work life** (Slack) is explicitly a judged, core part.
4. **Redemption exists conceptually** (swag/gift cards) but fulfillment details are **out of scope**.
5. **~4 hours** of work-time budget → depth over breadth.
6. **Personal GitHub repo**, submitted by **midnight CST July 20**.
7. Judged on **Effectiveness, Use of AI, Ease of Use, Maintainability, Presentation**.
