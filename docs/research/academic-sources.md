# Academic & Primary Sources

Sources behind `behavioral-research-report.md`, with quality rating and what each supports. All links are legitimate open-access or publisher pages (no Sci-Hub — decision D11). A dedicated academic-sources pass is queued to add DOIs and fill the gamification/anti-pattern gaps.

## Primary / peer-reviewed (motivation & recognition)

| Source | Supports | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Ryan & Deci (2000)** — *Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being*, American Psychologist | Autonomy/competence/relatedness; control-via-rewards backfires | https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf |
| **Ryan & Deci (2020)** — SDT update, *Contemporary Educational Psychology* | Reaffirms crowding-out; functional significance | https://stial.ie/resources/Ryan%20and%20Deci%202020%20self%20determination%20theory.pdf |
| **Murayama et al. (2010)** — *PNAS* fMRI study | Monetary reward undermines intrinsic motivation behaviorally + neurally | https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1013305107 |
| **Ryan, Ryan & Di Domenico (2019)** — retrospective on Deci (1971) | Task-contingent tangible rewards reduce free-choice engagement; verbal praise does not | https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/2019_RyanRyanDiDomencio_Deci1971.pdf |
| **Deci, Koestner & Ryan (2001)** — *Extrinsic Rewards and Intrinsic Motivation: Reconsidered Once Again* (128-study meta-analysis) | Tangible expected rewards reliably undermine; positive feedback enhances (d≈0.33) | https://www.selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2001_DeciKoestnerRyan.pdf |
| **USC CEO review** — *Negative Effects of Extrinsic Rewards* (incl. Fang & Gerhart 2012 β=.38) | Informational vs controlling; pay-for-performance can be positive in field | https://ceo.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/2013-05-G13-05-624-Negative_Effects_of_Extrinsic_Rewards.pdf |
| **Woolley & Fishbach** — *Immediate rewards increase intrinsic motivation* (Frontiers 2022) | Immediacy beats magnitude | https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.853879/full |
| **Bradler, Dur, Neckermann & Non (2016)** — *Employee Recognition and Performance: A Field Experiment*, Management Science | Non-monetary recognition raises performance ~5.2%; observer spillover; exclusivity caution | https://people.duke.edu/~dandan/webfiles/PapersPayoff/Scarcity_02June2015.pdf |
| **Frontiers in Psychology (2023, PMC10083470)** — witnessed-recognition diary study | Observer effect mediated by perceived organizational justice (manager→employee) | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10083470/ |
| **Frontiers in Psychology (2025)** — systematic review of workplace nudges | Combine nudges; low-cost/low-salience actions; leadership legitimacy | https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1556796/full |
| **Emerald / SHR** — power of peer-recognition points | Peer-recognition points programs (to mine in pass 2) | https://www.emerald.com/shr/article/24/1/2/1245460/ |
| **PMC11717283** — recognition & engagement (25,285 Korean employees) | Recognition strongest driver of engagement (β=0.394) | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11717283/ |

## Refuted in verification (do NOT cite as support)

- "Intrinsic and extrinsic motivation are positively correlated (.22), so no tradeoff" — **refuted 0-3**.
- "Witnessing recognition increases observer's own work engagement (B=0.101)" — **refuted 0-3**. (The *justice* mediation survived; the engagement spillover did not.)
